# AI Study Planner - Project Overview

## 1. Product Vision & Description
The **AI Study Planner** is a comprehensive, full-stack educational companion application designed to empower college students to manage their academic lives intelligently. It combines traditional study scheduling and progress tracking with advanced artificial intelligence to create highly personalized, adaptive timetables, predict exam readiness, analyze academic performance, and provide context-aware chat assistance.

## 2. Core Value Proposition
- **Adaptive Scheduling:** Replaces rigid, manual calendars with mathematical timetables that allocate study hours based on subject difficulty, exam proximity, and current grade averages.
- **Academic Insights:** Automatically translates raw test marks into performance snapshots, identifying weaker areas and recommending concrete improvement strategies.
- **AI-Powered Assistance:** Offers context-aware assistance, material summarization, and automatic document categorization to streamline study session preparation.
- **Motivational Support:** Provides daily motivational tips and dynamic topic suggestions to combat exam stress and study fatigue.

## 3. Target Audience
- **College/University Students:** Dealing with multiple courses, varying exam schedules, and complex syllabus requirements.
- **Self-Directed Learners:** Needing structure and structured topic-by-topic breakdowns for their subjects.
- **Students Preparing for Exams:** Seeking targeted revision strategies and motivational support.

## 4. Key Problems Solved
- **Rigid Schedules:** Most calendar tools do not adapt when a student falls behind or has an upcoming exam. This planner recalculates subject weights dynamically.
- **Lack of Actionable Analytics:** Students often do not know *which* subject needs the most attention. The performance snapshot highlights priority subjects.
- **Information Overload:** Long study materials are summarized in seconds, and files are automatically categorized under corresponding subjects.
- **Study Block:** When it is time to study, students often waste time deciding *what* to study. The planner suggests specific, bite-sized topics for each slot.

## 5. Business Goals
- **Freemium Model Adoption:** Engage users with core features (subject management, basic scheduling, and manual exam tracking) and drive conversion to Premium subscriptions for advanced features (AI-generated timetables, AI material summaries, and unlimited AI assistant chat).
- **Engagement and Retention:** Drive daily active usage through study streak tracking, active timetables, and notification reminders.
